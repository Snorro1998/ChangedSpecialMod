﻿[h1]Changed Special Mod[/h1]
This Terraria mod is about the game Changed by DragonSnow. It adds laboratories on the surface where you will find goo monsters, also know as latex beasts. They are quite strong and grow stronger with every boss you defeat.

[h2]Contents[/h2]
[list]
	[*]3 surface biomes
	[*]3 town NPCs
	[*]22 monsters
	[*]Some extra monsters on the drunk world
	[*]3 bosses
	[*]5 upgradeable weapons
	[*]3 mounts
	[*]Many furniture items
	[*]Transformation mechanic (WIP)
[/list]

[h2]Special Seeds[/h2]
These affect the world generation when used as the world seed. If you want variations of these seeds, just add letters or numbers after it.
[list]
	[*]blackgoozone: Covers the surface in black goo
	[*]whitegoojungle: Covers the surface in white goo
	[*]drydrydirt: Covers the surface in dry dirt
	[*]muchorange: Paints the world orange
[/list]

[h2]Mod Support[/h2]
This mod has built-in support for:
[list]
	[*]Biome Titles
	[*]Boss Checklist
	[*]Census
	[*]Fargos Mutant Mod
	[*]Music Display
	[*]Wikithis
[/list]

The mod also tries to avoid generating things in structures and biomes from these mods
[list]
	[*]Ancients Awakened
	[*]Bismuth
	[*]Calamity
	[*]Coralite
	[*]Mod of Redemption
	[*]Spirit and Spirit Reforged
	[*]Thorium
[/list]

Town NPCs can also sell herbs and fruit from the previously listed mods.

Mods that can cause issues:
[list]
	[*]Modliquid library: there is a bug in this mod that causes waterfalls to freeze in all modded biomes, included the Changed biomes.
	[*]MrPlague's Authentic Races: Not game-breaking, but the visuals of races are overwritten when transfurred.
[/list]


[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
This mod should work fine most of the time in multiplayer. In rare cases, you might see other players jittering while moving. This can mean that they are transfurred, but that it isn't visible for you. To fix this, they can use any syringe that makes the selection screen pop up. Syringes that don't do this can't fix this issue.


[h2]Other[/h2]
Most items and NPCs used to have scaled down sprites from Changed as placeholders. A lot of them have since been updated, but not all of them. You may come accross things that clash with Terraria's art style.
