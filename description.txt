This Terraria mod is about the game Changed by DragonSnow. It adds laboratories on the surface where you will find Latex beasts. They are quite strong and grow stronger with every boss you defeat.

-------- Contents ---------

- 5 biomes
- 3 Town NPCs
- 22 normal monsters
- Some extra monsters on the drunk world
- 3 Bosses
- 5 upgradeable weapons
- 3 mounts
- Many furniture items
- Transformation mechanic (WIP)


------- Mod support -------

This mod has built-in support for:
- Biome Titles
- Boss Checklist
- Census
- Fargos Mutant Mod
- Music Display
- Wikithis

Additionally, town npcs can sell fruits and herbs from these mods:
- Calamity
- Spirit and Spirit Reforged
- Thorium

Mods that can cause issues:
- Modliquid library: there is a bug in this mod that causes waterfalls to freeze in all modded biomes, included the Changed biomes.
- MrPlague's Authentic Races: Not game-breaking, but the visuals of races are overwritten when transfurred.

Most other mods should work fine. The only problem that you can run into is when the world generation overwrites structures or biomes from other mods and vice versa.


------- Multiplayer -------

Most things should work fine in multiplayer. In rare cases, you might encounter desycing of player transfurs. This means that they are transfurred, but you can't see it. A possible indicator of this happening is if the other player jitters on your screen when he moves. To fix this problem, he has to transfur into something else with any of the combined syringes (it's hard to explain, but it has to be a syringe that makes the interface pop up).


---------- Other ----------

Most items and NPCs used to have scaled down sprites from Changed as placeholders. A lot of them have since been updated, but not all of them. You may come accross things that clash with Terraria's art style.

--------- Changelog ---------

This update improves the conversion of normal and latex biomes, such as by preserving grass and trees.
It also makes some other blocks and walls infectable.

IMPORTANT: If you are updating a world with a city ruins biome in it, you need to replant the grass on the dirt with dry grass seeds.
They can be bought from Prototype.

0.5.6

Biomes
- Added black and white infected variants for the surface biomes
- Desert: Slimesand Dunes
- Jungle: Goo Jungle
- Snow: Goo Glacier

Items
- Added celebration cake which can be bought from Puro. Thank you all for 700+ downloads
- The candles of the happy birthday music box flicker and give off light
- Added dry grass seeds which can be used to plant grass on dry dirt
- Changed shimmering of crystals to: red -> green -> white -> white pillar -> red
- Added black and white goo materials. They can be used to make all the latex blocks using the solidifier
- Added black and white goo lanterns. They can be created with goo using the solidifier
- Dry dirt can't be turned into dirt anymore with the extractinator. Instead it can be crafted into dirt near water
- Reduced the price of document paper from 1 gold to 15 silver
- Removed bouncing sound of the basketball

Ambient entities
- Background objects that you can sometimes see in the sky, such as the hot air balloon and birds
- Yufeng Cub and plushies hanging from balloons
- They have a small chance to appear anywhere, or always in a latex biome

Latex spread
- Added infected surface biomes for: Jungle, Desert and Snow
- Trees can be infected and will no longer get destroyed
- Latex can spread to most blocks in these biomes
- Natural walls can be infected and unlike vanilla, these walls can also infect blocks

Clentiminator
- Crystals can be destroyed
- Purple, Red and Blue solutions can be used to remove latex biomes

NPCs
- Prototype sells all grass seeds including the new dry grass seeds used to spread the city ruins.
- Puro no longer hates the desert. Instead he dislikes the white latex biomes for him 'standing out too much'
- Puro no longer sells latex blocks. Instead he sells black and white goo to make them with
- Puro no longer sells dry dirt. It can now be crafted with dirt at a furnace
- Dr K no longer hates the jungle. Instead he dislikes the black latex biomes for him 'standing out too much'
- Devourer of Oranges can no longer spawn if there are town NPCs nearby
- Feng Yu can only appear in the Slimesand dunes
- Snow Leopard can only appear in the Goo Glacier

World generation
- Moved the generation tasks further back. This means that pots no longer generate in labs
- Document paper can appear on the walls in the laboratory with text on it

Resprited
- Biome backgrounds
- Debug teleport items
- Feng Yu (cleanup)
- Locker

Other
- Reduced chance of Changed game tips appearing on the loading screen
- Improved some translations

Fixes
- Puro and Dr K not immune to latex beasts
- More than 1 maintenance robot could appear
- The NPC portrait system caused errors when viewing or editing signs
- Latex beasts had a 0 percent spawn chance on most infected blocks if the player was not in the biome
- Crystals could grow everywhere and destroy pots and decals
- Debug teleport items could teleport players onto some pressure plates, detonators or into water and honey
