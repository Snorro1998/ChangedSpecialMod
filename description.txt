This Terraria mod is about the game Changed by DragonSnow. It adds laboratories on the surface where you will find goo monsters, also know as latex beasts. They are quite strong and grow stronger with every boss you defeat.
The mod is highly configurable, allowing you to change or disable content however you like.

-------- Contents ---------

- 5 biomes
- 3 Town NPCs
- 22 normal monsters
- Some extra monsters on the drunk world
- 3 Bosses
- 9 normal weapons and 5 upgradable weapons
- 4 mounts
- Many furniture items
- Transfur mechanic
- Custom liquids (optional, requires Modliquid Library)
- NEW: An underground laboratory, which is like a mini playthrough of Changed


------ Special Seeds -------
These affect the world generation when used as the world seed. If you want variations of these seeds, just add letters or numbers after it.
- blackgoozone: Covers the surface in black goo
- whitegoojungle: Covers the surface in white goo
- drydrydirt: Covers the surface in dry dirt
- muchorange: Paints the world orange


------- Mod support -------

This mod has built-in support for:
- Biome Titles
- Boss Checklist
- Census
- Fargos Mutant Mod
- Music Display
- Wikithis

The world generator tries to avoid placing things close to structures and biomes from these mods
- Ancients Awakened
- Bismuth
- Calamity
- Coralite
- Mod of Redemption
- Spirit and Spirit Reforged
- Spooky Mod
- Thorium
- Ultranium

In addition, town NPCs can sell herbs and fruit from the previously listed mods. 

Modliquid Library
If you want to have custom liquids in caves, you need to enable Modliquid Library manually, unless 
another mod requires it and already did this. The reason it is disabled by default is because 
it has a visual bug with waterfalls. If you enable it, the best thing you can do is to restart Terraria 
so you won't run into any strange issues such as the liquids not generating in a new world or them looking 
like normal water.

MrPlague's Authentic Races
Any race mod that depends on it will work, but their visuals are overwritten if you are transfurred.


------- Multiplayer -------

This mod should work fine most of the time in multiplayer. In rare cases, you might see other players jittering while moving. This can mean that they are transfurred, but that it isn't visible for you. To fix this, they can use any syringe that makes the selection screen pop up. Syringes that don't do this can't fix this issue.


---------- Other ----------

Most items and NPCs used to have scaled down sprites from Changed as placeholders. A lot of them have since been updated, but not all of them (like bosses). You may come accross things that clash with Terraria's art style.

--------- Changelog ---------


0.5.8.1

This update adds liquid latex, fireworks, a new magic weapon and resprites a couple of things.

Items
- Buffed Shredder and Mega Mauler by giving them a bleeding effect
- Added Carton of Orange Juice. It is a magic weapon that creates waves of orange juice
- Added fireworks rockets with shapes of vanilla and mod NPCs

Liquid latex
- Added black and white liquid latex in the underground. These liquids are optional and require you to enable "modliquid library". 
- The reason they are not enabled by default is because there is still a bug modliquid library that can break waterfall animations.
- Can be collected with a bucket or soaked up with an ultra absorbant sponge
- Buckets can be upgraded to bottomless variants with hallowed bars
- A bucket with black liquid can be shimmered into one with white liquid and vice versa
- It can infect blocks that are in direct contact with it
- Latex beasts move much quicker in liquids that are the same color as they are
- Players are slowed down by it like honey

Resprited
- Dr K. Also added all his missing animations (attack, sitting, talking, blinking and shaking hands)
- Crimclaw and its upgrades
- Dreadhorn and its upgrades
- Snow Leopard (a little bit of cleanup)

NPCs
- Dr K sells fireworks during a party

World generation
- World generator will try to avoid the area near spawn, unless it could not find any suitable place elsewhere
- Chest in shrines can no longer contain a mace. Instead they can contain the new magic weapon
- Underground labs try to generate in normal caves and will only generate in the desert, jungle or snow if it could not find a suitable place.

Fixed
- Labs could sometimes generate deep down in corruption shafts
- Town NPCs could talk about the same NPC that they were talking to
- Slipping sound played for all players instead of the local player (your own character)
- Underground latex biomes could generate anywhere and did not check for biomes or structures from other mods.

Mod calls
- Added "SetNPCEvo". You can use this to make two of the same NPCs merge into something else, like the goops do

Other
- Updated the wiki
