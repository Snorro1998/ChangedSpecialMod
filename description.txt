This Terraria mod is about the game Changed by DragonSnow. It adds laboratories on the surface where you will find goo monsters, also know as latex beasts. They are quite strong and grow stronger with every boss you defeat.
The mod is highly configurable, allowing you to change or disable content however you like.

-------- Contents ---------

- 5 biomes
- 3 Town NPCs
- 22 normal monsters
- Some extra monsters on the drunk world
- 3 Bosses
- 5 upgradeable weapons
- 3 mounts
- Many furniture items
- Transformation mechanic


------ Special Seeds -------
These affect the world generation when used as the world seed. If you want variations of these seeds, just add letters or numbers after it.
- blackgoozone: Covers the surface in black goo
- whitegoojungle: Covers the surface in white goo
- drydrydirt: Covers the surface in dry dirt
- muchorange: Paints the world orange


------- Mod support -------

This mod has built-in support for:
- Biome Titles
- Boss Checklist
- Census
- Fargos Mutant Mod
- Music Display
- Wikithis

The mod also tries to avoid generating things in structures and biomes from these mods
- Ancients Awakened
- Bismuth
- Calamity
- Coralite
- Mod of Redemption
- Spirit and Spirit Reforged
- Thorium
- Ultranium

Town NPCs can also sell herbs and fruit from the previously listed mods.

Mods that can cause issues:
- Modliquid library: there is a bug in this mod that causes waterfalls to freeze in all modded biomes, included the Changed biomes.
- MrPlague's Authentic Races: Not game-breaking, but the visuals of races are overwritten when transfurred.


------- Multiplayer -------

This mod should work fine most of the time in multiplayer. In rare cases, you might see other players jittering while moving. This can mean that they are transfurred, but that it isn't visible for you. To fix this, they can use any syringe that makes the selection screen pop up. Syringes that don't do this can't fix this issue.


---------- Other ----------

Most items and NPCs used to have scaled down sprites from Changed as placeholders. A lot of them have since been updated, but not all of them (like bosses). You may come accross things that clash with Terraria's art style.

--------- Changelog ---------


0.5.8

I want to thank you all for 1000 downloads. This is the first mod I have made for Terraria, 
so I am happy to see that despite some of its flaws, it is still well received.

This update is a bigger than most of the previous ones to celebrate the occasion.
It adds a large underground lab section, consisting of 6 rooms. 
Each room respresents a different part of a normal Changed playthrough. 
You can enter this underground section from a door in the basement of laboratories on the surface. 

Underground lab
- Consists of 6 different parts of Changed
- Black Goo Zone: Flickering lights
- Crystal Zone: Slippery blocks
- Library: White Tail spawns here if you haven't defeated thim yet
- Vents: Dark and difficult to navigate
- Gas room: Stand under the fans to avoid the blue gas
- Water room: Flip the switches to turn on the generators and the lights

General
- Flying oranges make less noise if there are many of them

Blocks
- Infected ice blocks no longer makes you slide around weirdly
- Laboratory tiles can no longer be blown up with bombs

World Generation
- Added biome variants for underground shrines

World Generation
- World generator now tries to avoid generating things in biomes from these mods:
- Ultranium
- Spooky Mod

Fixed
- World generator often spammed sounds (brrrrrrt), because of the way it placed wires
- World generator dug out too much space when a lab generated inside the terrain
- Camera moving smoothly when using an elevator in multiplayer, but not in single player
- Enemies using the custom FigtherAI did not fall through platforms if the player was below them
- Added missing map entries for a few tiles
